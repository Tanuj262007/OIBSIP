04 / HOW IT WORKS

Complex science.
Simple experience.

Tempora converts your input to Celsius internally, then calculates the equivalent value in every supported unit.

°C → °F(°C × 9/5) + 32↗
°F → °C(°F − 32) × 5/9↗
°C → K°C + 273.15↗
K → °CK − 273.15↗
